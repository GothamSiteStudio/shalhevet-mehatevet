App terms of use

Last updated: October 27, 2026

These terms apply to using the Shalhevet Mehatevet (שלהבת מחטבת) app, on a phone or in a browser. I wrote them short and plain. By using the app, you agree to them.

What the app is

The app is where I, Shalhevet Mehatevet, coach my trainees: workout and nutrition plans, tracking, a weekly check-in, and messages. There is no self sign-up: I open an account for each trainee who starts coaching with me. The app is for women aged 18 and over.

There are no payments in the app. The price, length, and cancellation terms of the coaching are agreed between us directly, outside the app.

Health and safety

  • The app is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.
  • The plans and menus are fitness and nutrition coaching. They are not medical advice, and they do not replace a doctor, a dietitian, or any other health professional.
  • Before you start or change a workout or nutrition plan, it is a good idea to consult a doctor. This matters especially during pregnancy and after giving birth, with an illness, an injury, or regular medication, or if you have had an eating disorder in the past.
  • Tell me about any health condition that could affect your workouts or nutrition, and also when something changes.
  • If you feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, or anything unusual, stop the workout and seek medical advice.
  • Every body responds differently. I coach you with care and professionalism, but no particular result can be guaranteed.

Your account

  • Your account is personal. Don't share your password with anyone.
  • If you think someone else has signed in to your account, change your password and let me know.
  • Keep your details accurate, so I can tailor your plan.

Fair use

  • The plans, menus, recipes, and texts I write are for your personal use. Please don't publish or sell them without my permission.
  • Do not try to access other trainees' information, break into the system, or disrupt it.
  • Let's keep our messages respectful.

Some of the images and descriptions in the exercise library come from the open free-exercise-db dataset, which its creators released into the public domain. Links to videos open on external sites, and those sites' own terms apply.

I do my best to keep the app working at all times, but there may be outages or maintenance from time to time. I may add, change, or remove features. If I decide to stop running the app, I will let you know in advance, as far as possible.

Ending your use

You can stop using the app and delete your account at any time (how to delete your account). When coaching ends, or if the app is misused, I may deactivate or delete the account. I will let you know before deleting it.
