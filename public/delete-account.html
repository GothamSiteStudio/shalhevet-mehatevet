Delete your account in the app

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the Shalhevet Mehatevet (שלהבת מחטבת) app, together with all of its information. You can delete it yourself from within the app, or ask me to, even without opening the app.

Delete it from within the app

The app is in Hebrew, so the button names are given in Hebrew with their meaning.

  1. Sign in to the app on your phone, or in a browser at app.shalhevetmehatevet.com (opens in a new tab).
  2. Go to the פרופיל (Profile) tab in the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the page and tap מחיקת חשבון (Delete account).
  4. In the window that opens, tap מחקי חשבון (Delete account) to confirm.

The account is deleted right away. When it is done, the message החשבון נמחק (The account was deleted) appears and the app returns to the sign-in screen.

If a message says the account was not deleted, or that the deletion was not completed, try again in a moment, or write to me and I will complete the deletion myself.

Please note: uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

My own coach account can't be deleted from inside the app. It is deleted at my request by whoever maintains the app for me.

Ask me, without opening the app

Can't sign in, already uninstalled the app, or haven't accepted the privacy policy on your first sign-in yet? Send me a request and I will delete the account for you.

  • Request deletion on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab)
  • Request deletion by email

You can also write directly to Shely.siyonov1@gmail.com. Please include your name and the email address on the account. It helps to write from the phone number or email address on the account, so I can make sure the request really comes from you. If needed, I will ask you a short question to confirm.

I will delete the account within 7 days at the latest, and send you a message once the deletion is complete.

What is deleted

Everything. Deleting the account deletes the account and all the information linked to it:

  • Account and sign-in details: name, email, phone, password, age, height, weight, goal, and activity level
  • Weigh-ins and body measurements
  • Your food diary, water, and cardio
  • Workouts, habits, and weekly check-ins, the photos you attached to workouts, and the exercises you starred
  • All the messages in our conversation, yours and mine, your updates, and meeting requests
  • The plans I built for you and the notes I wrote about your coaching
  • The record of your consent to the privacy policy
  • The phones registered for notifications

Deletion is final, and the information cannot be recovered. If you come back to coaching in the future, I will open a new account for you.

What is not deleted, and why

  • Backup copy: if a backup copy of the database exists, the information stays in it for up to 7 days and is then deleted automatically. The backup is only used to recover from a failure.
  • Things outside the app: WhatsApp chats, emails, and an inquiry you left in the website's contact form (it is deleted automatically after 90 days). If you like, write to me and I will delete what I have on my side too.
  • Invoices and receipts for coaching payments, if any. They are not in the app, and the law requires me to keep them.

Just need a break?

If you want to stop for a while without losing your information, you can simply sign out (in the פרופיל (Profile) tab, יציאה מהחשבון (Sign out)), or ask me to deactivate your account. A deactivated account is kept as it is, and it can't be signed in to until I reactivate it.
