FAQ

What's the difference between the two programs?

Full personal coaching includes a personalized meal plan, a personal gym training plan or access to the Zoom workouts, community, and my full availability on WhatsApp. The Zoom program is workouts and community, with no meal plan or personal coaching.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. The Zoom workouts are made for beginners who aren't ready to commit yet. And if you're ready to commit, full personal coaching gives you a meal plan and training plan tailored to you.

I don't have a gym membership. Can I still join?

Yes. You join the Zoom workouts from your living room, or even on vacation. Full personal coaching doesn't require a gym either: you can choose the Zoom workouts instead of the gym training plan.

I want to lose weight. Is this just another diet?

No. This isn't a diet. It's new habits. With personal coaching, your meal plan is built around your goal: losing weight, or gaining weight and toning up like I did.

How much does it cost?

I go over the prices of both programs in the intro chat. It's short and free, with no obligation, and you only decide whether to continue afterward.
