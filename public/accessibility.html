Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 2026

This site was built so that everyone can use it comfortably, including with a keyboard alone or with the page zoomed in.

How the site was built

The site was built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA and Israeli Standard 5568. In practice, that means:

  • A clear, semantic page structure
  • Everything can be operated with the keyboard alone
  • A visible focus indicator, so you can always see where you are on the page
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background
  • Text alternatives for images
  • A responsive layout that adapts to your screen and works with zoom up to 200%
  • Reduced-motion support: if your device is set to reduce motion, the site's animations are switched off
  • Form fields with labels and clear error messages
  • Correct handling of right-to-left (Hebrew) and left-to-right (English) text

How it was checked

The site was checked in October 2026 with automated tools (axe, pa11y, and Lighthouse) and with manual keyboard testing.

The coaching is online

The coaching itself takes place online only. There is no physical location to visit.

Services outside this site

WhatsApp, Instagram, and Zoom are third-party services. They are outside this site's control, and that includes their accessibility.
