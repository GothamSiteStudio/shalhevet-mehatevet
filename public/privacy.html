Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This site and the Shalhevet Mehatevet (שלהבת מחטבת) app are run by Shalhevet Mehatevet. Here it is in plain words: what information reaches me, what it is for, who else sees it, and what your rights are.

Who is responsible for your information

I, Shalhevet Mehatevet, a fitness and nutrition coach who works with women online, am responsible for the information collected on this site and in the app. You can send any privacy question or request straight to me, in any of the ways listed at the end of this page.

The website, shalhevetmehatevet.com

What the contact form collects

When you send the contact form, I receive:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • The program you're interested in
  • A note, if you choose to add one
  • The language of the page and the time you sent the form

Please don't include sensitive medical details in the note.

What your details are used for

Only to get back to you about your inquiry. Your details are not sold, not shared for advertising, and not added to a mailing list.

How your inquiry is handled

Your inquiry is delivered to me by email. A backup copy is kept with the site's hosting provider, Cloudflare, for up to 90 days and is then deleted automatically.

Your IP address is used briefly to prevent abuse of the form. It is not saved with your inquiry.

WhatsApp and Instagram

If you contact me through WhatsApp or Instagram, those platforms' own policies apply.

Analytics and cookies

The site uses Matomo, an open-source analytics tool hosted by the site's developer, Gotham Site Studio. It shows, in aggregate, how the site is used: which pages are viewed, the type of device and browser, the site you came from, your approximate region, and which buttons are used (for example, a tap on the WhatsApp button or sending the form).

Matomo sets first-party cookies to tell visits apart. There are no advertising cookies and no social-media tracking cookies on this site.

The Shalhevet Mehatevet app

The app is where I coach my trainees: workout and nutrition plans, tracking, a weekly check-in, and messages. It runs on phones (iPhone and Android), and there is also a browser version at app.shalhevetmehatevet.com. The app is in Hebrew. There is no self sign-up: I open an account for each trainee who starts coaching with me. Using the app is also subject to the terms of use.

What the app stores

  • Account details: name, email, phone, age, height, weight, goal, and activity level. I fill in some of them when I open your account
  • Weigh-ins and body measurements
  • Your food diary, water intake, and cardio minutes
  • Workouts you log, habits, and your answers to the weekly check-in
  • Our messages, your updates, and meeting requests
  • The plans I build for you: nutrition, weekly menu, workouts, goals, and habits
  • Notes I write for myself about your coaching. They are not shown to you in the app, but I will show them to you if you ask
  • When you last updated, so I know when to check in on you
  • When you accepted the privacy policy, and which version
  • If you allowed notifications on your phone: your phone's address in the notification service, and the phone type (iPhone or Android)

Your password is not stored as is. Only a one-way encrypted code (a hash) of it is kept, so no one, not even me, can see it.

Weight, measurements, and a food diary are personal, sensitive information. Please only write medical details in the app when they matter for your coaching, for example an injury your workouts need to take into account.

What the app does not do

  • No ads, and no tools that track what you do in the app
  • It does not use your location, camera, microphone, or contacts
  • It does not access the photos in your gallery. It only adds the weekly menu to it, when you choose to save it
  • No payments. We arrange payment for coaching directly, outside the app
  • Your information is not sold, not shared for advertising, and not used for anything other than your coaching

What the information is used for

  • To coach you: building and adjusting your plan, following your progress, answering your messages and check-ins, and scheduling meetings
  • To run the app: signing in, password resets, security, and fixing problems

You are not legally required to give me this information. But without your account details and your consent the app can't be used, and without the updates you log it is hard for me to tailor your coaching.

Phone notifications

When I send you a message, the app can tell you about it with a notification on your phone, and when you write to me, I get one too. Notifications only arrive if you allowed them when the app asked. A notification shows the sender's name and the start of the message, like WhatsApp.

  • You can turn notifications off, or hide the message text on the lock screen, at any time in your phone's settings.
  • When you sign out, your phone is removed from the notification list. When your account is deleted, all of its phones are deleted with it. A phone where the app has not been opened for six months leaves the list on its own.
  • The browser version has no notifications.

Who sees the information

  • Me, your coach. I see all the information in your account.
  • The person who maintains the app for me can access it, only to operate the app and fix problems.
  • Other trainees see none of your information. Even a message I send to several trainees at once reaches each of them separately, in her own conversation.

I rely on a few service providers to run the app. Each one receives only what it needs to provide its service:

  • Supabase: the database, sign-in, and password reset emails. All the information described above is stored there, on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Vercel: hosts the browser version and sends the app's files to your browser. Your information itself travels directly between your browser and Supabase.
  • Expo: builds the phone app, delivers updates to it, and delivers notifications to your phone. For a notification it receives your phone's address and what the notification shows (the sender's name and the start of the message), and passes them to Apple or Google. When the app checks for an update, it sends Expo a random installation ID, the device type, the app version, and the IDs of the updates it has, including any update that failed to open. These IDs are not linked to your name or your account.
  • jsDelivr: a public file delivery service that the exercise images load from. Like any site that shows an image, it sees your device's IP address while the image loads, and it receives nothing from your account.
  • Apple and Google: the stores you download the app from. Their own policies apply to the download. They may pass me aggregated figures, such as the number of downloads, and crash reports, depending on the settings you chose on your device. They also deliver notifications to your phone: on iPhone through the Apple Push Notification service, and on Android through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Vercel and Expo are US companies, and so are the notification services of Apple and Google, so some of the information described for them is transferred outside Israel and the European Union. I only work with providers that commit to protecting the information at a level no lower than described here, and to using it only to provide their service. Other than that, I will only share information with anyone else if the law requires me to.

Keeping the information secure

  • All communication between the app and the servers is encrypted (HTTPS), and the information itself is stored encrypted in the database.
  • The database is built so that each trainee can only access her own information.
  • On a phone, your sign-in details are kept in the device's encrypted storage. In a browser they are kept in your browser, so remember to sign out on a shared computer.
  • Access to the information is limited to me and to the person who maintains the app for me.

No system is completely secure. If a security incident affects your information, I will act as the law requires and let you know. You can help too: choose a password you don't use anywhere else, and don't share it with anyone.

How long the information is kept

  • For as long as your account exists.
  • When coaching ends, I may deactivate your account. You can no longer sign in, but the information is kept so you can pick up where you left off if you come back. If you don't plan to come back, you can delete the account yourself before coaching ends, or ask me to delete it at any time.
  • When the account is deleted, the information is deleted from the database immediately and permanently. If a backup copy of the database exists, the information stays in it for up to 7 days and is then deleted automatically.

Your consent

The first time you open the app, it shows you a short explanation of what is stored and who sees it, with a link to this page. You can start using the app only after you accept. The app records when you accepted and which version of the policy.

When something important changes in the policy, you will be asked to accept it again. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or asking me to delete it.

Age

The app and the coaching are for women aged 18 and over. I don't open accounts for anyone under 18.

Deleting your app account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app: in the פרופיל (Profile) tab, at the bottom of the page, tap מחיקת חשבון (Delete account). This deletes the account and all of its information, and it cannot be undone. You can also ask me to delete your account on WhatsApp or by email, without opening the app.

How to delete your account and what is deleted: full details

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see the information kept about you, to correct it, or to delete it. This applies both to inquiries from the website and to your app account.

  • See: most of the information is shown to you in the app itself. I will give you the rest when you ask.
  • Correct: you can update your name, age, height, phone, and goal yourself on the Profile screen. I will correct anything else at your request.
  • Delete: from within the app, or by asking me (how to delete your account).

To protect your information, I may check that a request really comes from you, for example by the phone number or email address on the account.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version. When something important changes for the app, you will be asked to accept the policy again the next time you sign in.
